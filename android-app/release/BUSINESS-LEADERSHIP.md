## Teaching and assessments

The old course delivered 565 question entries from just 54 unique prompts. The new
bank has 617 distinct authored items: four for each of 143 teaching topics, 15
skill supplements and 30 project scenarios. A topic's first three items are practice
and its fourth is reserved for assessment. All 565 delivered questions are distinct,
with four choices and explanatory feedback. The correct choice appears across all
four positions. Repeated topic titles use explicit module context and different
subject matter, including growth strategy, performance reviews, continuous
improvement and digital transformation.

The module Leadership Assessment and six subject papers each contain eight items.
They examine their actual subject: fundamentals, strategy, finance, marketing,
team leadership and change. The two fundamentals papers are separate. The midterm
covers every module from 1 through 10; the 20-question final covers every teaching
module, 1 through 17. Capstone evaluation and portfolio review use separate
15-question workplace-scenario papers. Graduation uses 15 further reserved items.
No paper repeats a practice question or a question already issued in another paper.
Allocation is deterministic, idempotent and fails on missing or insufficient content.
Existing completed study records remain; question fingerprints reject obsolete
unfinished drafts.

All 143 teaching notes are rewritten with definitions, topic distinctions, worked
examples, review questions, activities and assignments. Twenty particularly useful
subjects have expanded examples, including SWOT, cash forecasts, budgeting,
conversion rates, negotiation, KPI definitions and AI evaluation. Review choices
are visible and answers reveal on demand. All 21 projects now have specific
required deliverables and a common evidence/feasibility/responsibility rubric.
Notes use module-qualified keys, readable tables and the TIH light palette. The
native reader retains script, file and network restrictions. The course description
now identifies written template outlines without promising absent downloadable files.

Employment and compliance examples require checking current applicable local rules
and seeking qualified advice for unclear decisions. Financial examples distinguish
profit from cash and label simplified assumptions. AI examples require relevant
evaluation, approved information handling and accountable human decisions.

## Verification and limits

Source checks verify all topic mappings, distinct prompts, answer validity, relevant
subject pools, full-syllabus coverage, separate project scenarios and allocation
failure behavior. Export checks compare the app course with the shared source,
verify ninth catalog position and check artwork and all video assignments. Local
browser checks use the real player with a synthetic learner and no external network,
exercise all subject papers and specialist practice, and verify rendered feedback.
Reader checks use 360-pixel width at 18- and 24-pixel reading sizes. Android device
tests cover the overview, SWOT and cash-flow practices, saved graduation results,
draft clearing and the real offline reader. The expanded device suite has a
five-minute instrumentation limit rather than four minutes.

Video-identity checks do not establish current playback of every external video.
The APK is a signed preview, not a Play Store publication.
