## Assessment correction

The prior import delivered 580 question entries but only 523 distinct prompts.
Its authored bank had three items per topic and one repeated fixed-cost prompt;
broad exams reused practice questions, while small subject tests used generic
pools. The corrected bank has four items per teaching topic, 664 distinct items
in total. Each topic's first three form the practice; its fourth is examination-only.
All 580 delivered questions are distinct, with four options and explanations.
No assessment repeats a practice item or another issued assessment item.

Existing topic-specific practice is retained with targeted corrections to
ambiguity, fixed-cost range, grant conditions, structure changes and IP protection.
Legal and tax questions avoid presenting another jurisdiction's rules as local
requirements. Reference foundations include the SBA business-structure guide,
Liberia's Ministry of Commerce/Business Registry and Revenue Authority, and WIPO
copyright guidance. They are conceptual teaching checks, not an individual legal,
tax or investment recommendation.

Four eight-question subject tests cover entrepreneurship foundations, marketing,
finance and legal topics. The 15-question midterm covers Modules 1–10, the
20-question final covers all 18 teaching modules, and graduation has 15 questions.
The allocator is deterministic, idempotent and rejects missing topics or
insufficient assessment material. Existing completed records remain; the current
question fingerprint discards outdated unfinished attempts. Loader versions are
updated for website caches.
