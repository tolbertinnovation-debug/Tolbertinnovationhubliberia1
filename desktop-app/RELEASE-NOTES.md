TIH Learning Desktop 0.19.0 — TIH website theme and expanded lesson reading.

Deep blue #002868, TIH red #E31E24, white and pale blue surfaces now match the website palette. Navigation, primary actions, the welcome screen and the learning room share the TIH theme.

The lesson list starts closed to give reading and video more width. Open Lessons or My notes when needed. As learners scroll down, the app header and large lesson title step aside while a compact toolbar keeps Lessons, My notes, Focus view and Full Screen accessible, giving the lesson more vertical room. Scroll to the top or choose Course controls to bring them back. Video and lesson frames stay mounted during this change. Wheel, PageUp/PageDown, saved reading positions and focus view remain supported.

Existing courses, accounts and progress stay compatible. Back up in Settings and close the app before installing. This build provides a tested Windows installer as a workflow artifact.
