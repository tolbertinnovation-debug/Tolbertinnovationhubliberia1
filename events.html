Before You Join

Questions & Participation Details

Are all programmes free?

Resource browsing, course access, consultations, and live sessions have different terms. Check the linked programme page and ask TIH to confirm charges before enrolling or making a payment.

Where are the live events held?

The date, online link, physical venue, and accessibility arrangements must be confirmed for each live session. The session-interest cards do not announce a confirmed event.

Can a school, NGO, or church request a group session?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe the audience, topic, expected group size, and preferred format. TIH will review feasibility and availability.

Does a funding reminder mean I am eligible?

No. Review the official provider’s applicant rules, project requirements, deadline, and required documents. Funding decisions are made by the provider.

How do I follow updates?

Visit TIH News, check the programme’s own page, or contact our team for current session information.
