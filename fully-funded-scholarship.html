China · Peking University

Yenching Academy Fellowship

Master’s in China Studies

Funding

Tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend, one return journey, and field study costs. Second-year fellowship support requires continued eligibility and a separate application.

Who can apply?

Liberian applicants can apply as non-Chinese citizens. A bachelor’s degree by enrolment, strong academics, English proficiency, and an interest in China are required. Partner-university applicants must follow their university’s pre-selection process.

Application timing

2027 intake deadline: 30 November 2026 at 09:00 Beijing time (01:00 in Liberia). Submit through the official Yenching application portal.

United Kingdom · University of Oxford

Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships and Leadership Programme

Eligible graduate courses

Funding

Full course fees and a living-cost grant for the agreed period of fee liability, plus a leadership development programme.

Who can apply?

Liberia is listed as an eligible country of ordinary residence. Applicants need an eligible Oxford course offer, financial need, and a plan to return and contribute to public life. Deferred offers are excluded.

Application timing

2027 entry: apply by your course’s December or January funding deadline. Select this scholarship in the graduate application and upload the required Weidenfeld-Hoffmann statement.

France · Sciences Po

Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at Sciences Po

Two-year Master’s

Funding

Covers the full financial needs of selected scholars throughout the two-year course, with academic and personal support.

Who can apply?

African citizenship, including Liberia; no non-African dual nationality. Applicants must be under 35 and meet the approved university, bridge/mentoring programme, or UNHCR refugee route. A first Master’s only; one-year and dual degrees are excluded.

Application timing

The provider lists October to mid-December 2026 for fee-waiver requests, with detailed dates to be published. Follow the scholarship and international graduate admission steps; admission alone does not secure funding.

United Kingdom · University of Oxford

Mastercard Foundation–AfOx Scholarships

Eligible taught Master’s

Funding

Course fees and a living-cost grant, with support for an economy return flight, visa, Immigration Health Surcharge, and settling in under the award terms.

Who can apply?

African citizens ordinarily resident in Africa, including eligible Liberian applicants. Meet Oxford admission requirements and check the current course list and scholarship selection criteria.

Application timing

Apply for the Oxford course by its main December or January funding deadline. Check the AfOx provider page for the next intake and any additional questionnaire or selection steps.

Ghana · African Institute for Mathematical Sciences

AIMS Ghana Structured Master’s Scholarships

Master’s in Mathematical Sciences

Funding

Full scholarships for accepted Structured Master’s students, including accommodation, food, and health insurance. Confirm travel arrangements with AIMS.

Who can apply?

Applicants from across Africa, including Liberia, with a qualifying mathematics, science, or engineering degree containing substantial mathematics. Academic strength, leadership, and community service are considered.

Application timing

The published 2026 deadline was 15 March 2026 and has passed. Prepare for the next call; use the official AIMS network application portal linked on the provider page.

Brunei Darussalam · Participating institutions

Government of Brunei Darussalam Scholarship (BDGS)

Diploma / Undergraduate / Master’s

Funding

Tuition and specified university fees, accommodation, personal and meal allowances, books, economy travel at the start and end of study, and insurance under the award terms.

Who can apply?

International applicants, including Liberians, must meet the chosen course’s entry and English requirements. Published age limits are 25 for diploma/undergraduate and 35 for Master’s on 1 July of the start year. Prior tertiary study in Brunei is excluded.

Application timing

The 2026/27 round closed on 15 February 2026. The next call is not confirmed here. Watch the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page; separate university applications may also be required.

Canada · McGill University

McCall MacBain Scholarships

Master’s / Eligible professional degrees

Funding

Full tuition and fees for an eligible degree, a living stipend during academic terms, relocation support, and leadership development. Partial finalist awards are separate from the full scholarship.

Who can apply?

International candidates, including Liberians, may apply. Meet the current bachelor’s completion/recency or age criteria, McGill’s degree and language requirements, and the scholarship’s leadership criteria.

Application timing

Applications for the 2027 cohort are closed. The deadline for applicants studying at universities outside Canada and the USA was 19 August 2026. Watch for the next announced cycle; McGill admission is a separate application.

Knight-Hennessy Scholars

Master’s / PhD / Eligible professional degrees

The 2027 cohort closed on 6 October 2026. Prepare for a later announced cycle; the scholarship and Stanford degree programme have separate applications and deadlines.

Clarendon Scholarships

Master’s / DPhil

Reach Oxford Scholarship

Undergraduate

Funding

Course fees, a living-cost grant, and one return airfare per year, normally for three or four years.

Who can apply?

Liberia is on the eligible nationality list. An Oxford admission offer, financial need, and social commitment are required. Medicine is excluded.

Application timing

2027 scholarship details are due in January 2027; the published scholarship deadline is 26 January 2027. You must already have applied for Oxford admission on time.

Canada · University of Toronto

Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship

Undergraduate

Funding

Tuition, books, incidental fees, and full residence support for four years. Do not assume airfare or all personal expenses are covered.

Who can apply?

International students entering a first undergraduate degree. A high-school nomination is required; students already attending post-secondary study are ineligible.

Application timing

2027 entry: school nominations closed 9 October 2026. Already nominated students must apply for admission by 16 October and submit the scholarship application by 6 November 2026.

Fulbright Foreign Student Program

Master's / PhD Fully Funded Liberia Eligible

  • Bachelor's degree with strong academic record

  • Leadership potential and community commitment

United States

SHE-CAN Scholarship

Undergraduate Full Ride Women Only Liberia Eligible

SHE-CAN (She Can, I Can) provides a full scholarship for young Liberian women to study at top US colleges. The program invests in future female leaders who commit to returning home and serving their country.

  • Liberian female student, ages 17–20
  • Strong academic record

  • Commitment to serve in Liberia for at least 3 years

Erasmus Mundus Scholarship

Master's Degree Fully Funded Open to All Nationalities

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Degrees are prestigious EU-funded programs that allow students to study at two or more European universities. Liberian students are highly encouraged to apply as the EU prioritizes students from partner countries.

South Korea

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS/KGSP)

Undergraduate & Master's Fully Funded Liberia Eligible

The Global Korea Scholarship (formerly KGSP), funded by the Korean Government, offers full scholarships for international students to study at Korean universities at both undergraduate and graduate levels. It includes Korean language training before studies begin.

What's Covered

  • Full tuition and enrollment fees
  • Monthly stipend (KRW 900,000–1,000,000)
  • Round-trip airfare
  • 1-year Korean language training
  • Medical insurance
  • Settlement and research allowances

Key Requirements

  • Citizen of a GKS partner country (Liberia ✓)
  • Under 25 (undergraduate) or under 40 (graduate) years of age
  • GPA of 2.64 / 4.0 or higher
  • Good health (medical examination required)

Japan

MEXT Japan Scholarship

Undergraduate & Postgraduate Fully Funded Liberia Eligible

The Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship is one of the most prestigious scholarships in Asia, covering studies at Japanese universities for both undergraduate and graduate students. Liberian students apply through the Embassy of Japan (accredited via Ghana).

What's Covered

  • Full tuition and examination fees
  • Monthly living allowance (¥117,000–145,000)
  • Round-trip airfare
  • Japanese language training before enrollment

Key Requirements

  • Liberian citizen aged 17–24 (undergraduate) or under 35 (graduate)
  • Strong academic record
  • Apply through Embassy of Japan (accredited to Liberia via Ghana)
  • Medical fitness certificate required

Turkey

Türkiye Burslari (Turkish Government Scholarship)

Undergraduate & Postgraduate Fully Funded Liberia Eligible

The Turkish Government Scholarship (Türkiye Burslari) is one of the most popular scholarships for African students, covering undergraduate, Master's, and PhD degrees at top Turkish universities. It includes free Turkish language training before studies begin.

What's Covered

  • Full tuition at a Turkish university
  • Monthly stipend (varies by level: UG/MA/PhD)
  • Free on-campus accommodation
  • Round-trip airfare
  • One year Turkish language course (free)
  • Health insurance

Key Requirements

  • Citizen of a non-Turkish country (Liberia ✓)
  • Age: under 21 (UG), under 30 (Master's), under 35 (PhD)
  • Strong academic record
  • Online application through official portal

Islamic Development Bank

IsDB Scholarship Program

Undergraduate — SPMC route Fully Funded Muslim Students Liberia Eligible

Muslim applicants from Liberia should check the IsDB Scholarship Program for Muslim Communities in Non-Member Countries (SPMC). This undergraduate route has specific community and financial-need requirements. Its support is an interest-free loan to the student, repayable to a local education trust under program terms.

What's Covered

  • Full tuition at approved university
  • Monthly living allowance
  • Travel support only where applicable under the award terms
  • Health insurance
  • Book and research allowances

Key Requirements

  • Muslim applicant from an eligible non-member community in Liberia
  • Meet the current SPMC age, academic, and community endorsement requirements
  • Strong academic record
  • Commitment to return and contribute to home country
