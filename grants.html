Find Grants, Student Aid, and Assistance Relevant to Liberia

Forty-eight additions were checked against official provider sources on 16 October 2026. International eligibility includes Liberia where the stated criteria are met. Older listings are programme resources with current intake unconfirmed, except calls marked closed. Programme budgets are not individual grant awards. Choose a route that fits your organization

NGOs: Check your legal status, leadership requirements, project focus, and the donor’s geographic rules.

Hospitals and clinics: Use Medical Donations for equipment and medicines. Check licensing, product availability, import clearance, co-financing, shipping, and maintenance requirements with the provider.

Patients and families: Use Patient Treatment Assistance for condition-specific referral routes. Contact the provider or treating clinician directly; these listings do not promise cash payments or coverage of every medical condition.

Churches and faith-based organizations: Check each donor’s denomination, project purpose, and endorsement rules. Catholic-only sources are labeled. Social-project donors may exclude worship and evangelism even when the applicant is a church.

Schools: Japan’s grassroots programme covers eligible education institutions; Rotary projects need a club sponsor. Theirworld supports nonprofits, while Spencer and Internet Society opportunities require research eligibility.

Businesses: D-Prize supports new ventures distributing proven interventions. DRK targets scalable social enterprises and may offer investment rather than a grant. Conventional businesses must meet each funder’s impact criteria.

Use the application-route filter to separate rolling opportunities, published deadlines, partner routes, and closed rounds to monitor.

Donation and grant routes for churches in Liberia

Pastoral work and church buildings: Check ACN or missio for qualifying Catholic projects. USCCB requests must go through an eligible bishops’ conference.

Community projects: Bread for the World considers eligible nonprofit development partners. UTO serves qualifying Episcopal / Anglican applicants. Waldensian funding supports social projects under its annual call.

Books and learning: Church Periodical Club has Episcopal / Anglican routes with fund-specific endorsements and overseas submission rules.

Before applying: Match the project to the donor’s purpose, confirm the denomination and applicant rules, and prepare registration, budget, financial records, and required Church endorsements. Awards are competitive.

Environmental NGO grants for Liberia

Forest and habitat protection: Waterloo supports avoided deforestation and community forest rights; overseas applicants need a qualifying reference. Rainforest Trust focuses on creating or expanding protected areas.

Climate and biodiversity: Monitor IKI Small Grants and CEPF for future calls. Their listed 2026 rounds are closed. Rufford and other conservation sources also appear under Environment.

Before applying: Prepare registration documents, a project map, community consent where required, measurable conservation outcomes, a budget, financial records, and any donor-required references.

Student financial aid and innovation project grants

Students: WMI supports qualifying first-degree study in your home country or region. MMEG’s listed route is for eligible women already studying in the US or Canada. MPOWER’s scholarship intake is paused. Check what each award covers, your remaining costs, and the study location.

Innovators: FID accepts qualifying organizational projects year-round, with restrictions by funding stage. The Awesome Conservation and Climate chapter offers small project grants. Also browse existing Emergent Ventures, Catalyst Fund, DRK, and D-Prize listings; match your project to each programme’s purpose.

Prepare your application: Students need academic records, a cost breakdown, and any required enrolment evidence or references. Innovation teams need a clear problem, target users, project milestones, evidence, and a budget. Follow each provider’s rules for original application writing.
