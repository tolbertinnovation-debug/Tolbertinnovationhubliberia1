How the three question types work

True / False / Not Given

True: the passage says it. False: the passage says the opposite. Not Given: the passage does not settle it either way. Most marks are lost by choosing False when the honest answer is Not Given. Ask yourself: could I point at the sentence that contradicts this? If not, it is Not Given.

Multiple choice

The wrong options are wrong for a reason: they reverse the passage, overstate it with always or only, or state something true in the world but absent from the text. Answer from the passage, never from what you already know.

Sentence completion

Copy the words exactly as printed, and never more than the limit. A synonym is marked wrong even when the meaning is right. Read the grammar around the gap first: it usually tells you whether a noun, verb or adjective is wanted.
