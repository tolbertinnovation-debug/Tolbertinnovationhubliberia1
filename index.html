What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These commitments guide how Tolbert Innovation Hub teaches, builds, communicates, and serves our community.

01

Transparency

We aim to explain our services, fees, eligibility requirements, and processes clearly, so people can make informed decisions.

02

Integrity

We commit to honest communication, accurate information, and ethical conduct. We represent opportunities fairly and avoid promises we cannot support.

03

Accountability

We take responsibility for our work, welcome feedback, and correct mistakes. We follow through on agreed commitments and explain changes.

04

Inclusion & Respect

We treat people with dignity and work to make learning and opportunities more accessible, across backgrounds, abilities, and circumstances.

05

Purposeful Innovation

We build and improve practical solutions to real problems, using technology to support learning, local businesses, and community needs.

06

Excellence

We value careful work, useful learning, and dependable service. We keep improving our content, products, and support through review and feedback.

07

Community Service

We put people and community needs at the center of our work, helping Liberians build skills, pursue opportunities, and contribute to development.

08

Collaboration

We value shared knowledge and respectful partnerships. We work with learners, organizations, and professionals toward clearly defined goals.

You apply online, our team reviews and approves your account, then you unlock any WASSCE subject with a one-time US$3 / 500 LRD registration fee, or standard courses for US$10 / 1900 LRD, by Mobile Money. The tuition itself is free. Learn at your own pace on any phone, and earn a verified certificate you can share with employers.

Liberian Law · TIH Learning Hub

Understand the law.
Build your legal skills.

The Complete Liberian Law Programme takes you from legal foundations to applied research, writing and courtroom simulations. Designed for prospective and current law students, community educators and professionals, it supports independent study of Liberia’s legal system.

Explore constitutional law, civil and criminal procedure, land rights, contracts, business law and professional ethics through full teaching notes, distinct video lessons and readings from Liberian primary sources.

  • Two stages: foundations and applied legal study
  • Practice quizzes, module assessments and examinations
  • Eight portfolio projects, including legal opinions and moot court

Before You Start

Questions Visitors Often Ask

Understand the process before you enrol, apply, or request a service.

Where should I start?

Choose your goal in the Start Here section. You can browse resources first, then use the programme page to apply, enquire, or contact TIH. Choose a starting point →

Are courses completely free?

The Learning Hub describes tuition-free learning with registration charges for access. Check the current price, entry requirements, and payment instructions for the specific course before paying. Check course details →

Does a scholarship or grant listing guarantee funding?

Awards are decided by the provider. Check eligibility, the current deadline, required documents, and covered expenses. Some entries are closed rounds to monitor or partner-based routes. Browse funding and aid →

Can TIH build a website or application for my organization?

Use the Business Project Intake Form to describe your goal, users, features, and timeline. The team can then discuss the scope, delivery milestones, support, and a project quote. Describe your project →

How can I use Grandpa AI?

Open Grandpa AI at libgrandpaai.com to explore the application. You can also read about the project on this website. Open Grandpa AI →

Where can hospitals or patients find assistance?

The grants directory has separate medical donation and patient-treatment routes. Check the provider’s criteria and contact instructions; availability depends on the service and the case. Explore health assistance →
