Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How much do the courses cost?
Applying is free. WASSCE subjects unlock for US$3 / L$500 each, and every other course for US$5 / L$950, paid by Mobile Money. IELTS prep is US$50, and TOEFL and SAT prep are US$25.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes. Every course is a complete program and you earn a verifiable Professional Certificate the moment you reach 100%, download it instantly, no waiting and no approval needed.
How do I become a member?
Register free, complete any 3 courses and earn their certificates, then share the certificates with us. We contact you and email your membership form and details, unlocking job placement, contracts, networking, free live training and mentorship.
Can I learn on my phone?
Absolutely. The whole platform is built for phones and works even on limited data, so you can learn from anywhere in Liberia.
How do I pay?
By Mobile Money. After you apply and log in, choose a course and pay; the TIH team confirms your payment and unlocks it for you.
Do I need any experience to start?
No. Most courses start at beginner level and guide you step by step, all the way to job-ready skills.
