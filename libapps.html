App download is free. Approved course access requires your TIH account.

Installation & preview information
  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser or file manager. You can turn that permission off afterward.
  3. Open TIH Learning and sign in with your existing TIH account.

This is a debug preview, outside Google Play. An unlocked device can expose stored account data. Use it for testing on a device you control. Back up study notes before removing an older app.

Download for Windows ↓

App download is free. Approved course access requires your TIH account.

Installation & preview information
  1. Download the installer on your Windows computer.
  2. Open the EXE and follow the setup instructions.
  3. Launch TIH Learning Desktop and sign in with your TIH account.

For Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. This is a preview installer; Windows may show a publisher warning. Back up your progress before updating.

Lessons and notes are available offline after verified access. Internet is needed for sign-in, periodic access checks and videos. macOS and Linux installers are not available.
