Learn through Liberia’s legal framework.

Study the Constitution, courts, statutes and legal methods through primary readings and fictional Liberian cases. Every taught lesson includes a distinct educational video, written explanations, a worked example, a glossary, review questions and an assignment.

Plan about 80 hours of lessons, readings, revision and portfolio work. Progress step by step through 106 learning activities, including 40 topic quizzes, 20 module checks, a midterm and a final examination.

  • Find and verify legal authority; write case briefs and IRAC analyses.
  • Connect constitutional rights, procedure and evidence to facts.
  • Analyse contracts, land, employment, family and business disputes.
  • Practise ethical advocacy and build a legal-study portfolio.
Independent law-school preparation

Subject coverage draws on publicly listed Liberian law-school courses. This TIH course carries no school affiliation or academic credit and does not confer a law degree or permission to practise law.

Put your learning into practice.

Each project has concrete deliverables, a completion checklist and a rubric. Keep your work in a portfolio and record your self-assessment in the course Notes.

Liberian Case Brief and Research Portfolio

Use one published Supreme Court opinion located through the Judiciary. Produce a case brief and a research log. Do not invent a case citation.

Contracts and Land Due-Diligence Portfolio

A fictional cooperative wishes to lease a rural workspace and buy equipment. Land authority and delivery terms are disputed.

Rights, Employment and Evidence Memorandum

A fictional worker is dismissed following an accusation based on a cropped message. There is a disputed medical record.

Moot Court Capstone and Reasoned Opinion

Represent each side in a fictional dispute about a supplier contract, corporate authority and defective performance. Then act as the classroom decision-maker.

Before you enrol.

Who is this course for?

Prospective law students, university learners, community educators and professionals seeking a structured foundation in Liberian legal study. No prior legal training is required. Careful English reading and writing are helpful.

Is this an accredited degree or official law-school course?

No. It is an independent TIH course and does not replace a degree at Liberia School of Law, Louis Arthur Grimes School of Law or another institution. Confirm admissions, degree requirements and professional qualification directly with the relevant institution and authorities.

How do assessment and certification work?

The player marks quizzes automatically. Complete lessons in order, pass the quizzes and final at the existing 70% threshold, and complete the project steps to unlock the TIH Certificate of Completion. Project completion is self-recorded with the supplied rubric; tutor marking is not promised unless separately arranged.

Can I rely on the course for a real legal matter?

The course teaches foundations and research methods. It is not personalised legal advice. Verify current law and obtain help from an authorised Liberian legal professional for a real case. Do not assume foreign video deadlines, procedures or remedies apply in Liberia.
