How We Work

Four Clear Steps to Get Started

  1. Choose your route

    Open the service page that matches your goal, or contact TIH if you are unsure where to begin.

    Tell us about your study goal, organization, project, or enquiry. Use the relevant programme’s document instructions.

Common Questions

Before You Request a Service

Are all TIH services free?

No. The library and some resource browsing are free, while courses can require registration and digital projects require a scope and quotation. Check the linked programme’s terms and confirm any service charges before paying.

Can TIH guarantee a scholarship, grant, admission, or visa?

TIH can provide guidance and resource links. The scholarship provider, donor, institution, or responsible authority makes the decision. Follow their official requirements.

What should I prepare for a software project discussion?

Describe your users, the problem, required features, existing systems, content, budget range, and preferred timeline. Ask about maintenance and ownership as part of the proposal.

Can my school, church, or NGO request group support?

Yes. Use the Events & Programs enquiry form to describe the topic and audience. TIH must confirm availability, format, and any fees.

How do I find services relevant to my organization?

Use the audience filter above, then review the “For” and “Start with” details on each card. Contact TIH if you need help choosing a route.
